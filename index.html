OUR STORY

STARTED AS A CONVERSATION.
BECAME A LEGACY.

Every piece is designed around real people and real experiences. The message is not manufactured. The story comes first.

Hood Tendencies began as a conversation—and a cultural wink. The phrase was born in 2014 during a lighthearted exchange between myself, Jaronika Dancy, and a former co-worker. What started as a play on slang became something much deeper: a reflection of the strength, survival, and sacred pride woven into our communities.

As a 44-year-old widowed mother of Jeremiah Jones (18) and one of five children raised by Patricia and the late Jerome Dancy, I’ve witnessed the power of legacy firsthand. In 2024, I reignited this vision not just as a clothing brand, but as a platform for family, faith, and generational voice.

Hood Tendencies isn’t about fashion trends—it’s about fashion with testimony. Every piece represents someone’s story. From my 66-year-old mother to my teenage son, our Family Capsule Collection features custom hoodies linked to real voices, each answering:

“WHY DO YOU HAVE
HOOD TENDENCIES?”

This brand honors where we come from—and reminds us who we’re becoming.

REAL TENDENCIES.

The Family Capsule Collection is where Hood Tendencies began. It’s more than a line of hoodies—it’s a living legacy. Each one featured here was designed in collaboration with someone from my family, and every quote, color, and fit reflects who they are and what they’ve lived through.

Some wear zip-ups for comfort, while others prefer pullovers or T-shirt hoodies—but no matter the style, the message is the same: this hood has a story.

These videos give you a glimpse into those stories. You’ll hear from my 67-year-old mother, my 18-year-old son, siblings, nieces, and cousins—each sharing their personal testimony of why they chose their saying, what it represents to them, and what it means to walk with hood tendencies.

This page is a tribute to the people who shaped this brand and an invitation to connect, reflect, and find the message that resonates with you.

When you’re ready, head to the shop and explore the full collection. Your story might be different—but your hood can still speak.
